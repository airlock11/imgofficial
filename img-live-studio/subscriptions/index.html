Subscription, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Pre-publication draft prepared November 3, 2026. No effective date has been set.

DRAFT FOR LEGAL AND OPERATIONAL REVIEW — NOT YET EFFECTIVE. Do not publish until the service operator, billing arrangements, platform capabilities and relevant laws have been verified.

This policy applies when paid IMG LIVE Studio subscriptions are offered. Billing and purchase channels are not yet verified for publication. Actual plan price, currency, renewal cycle, trial terms (if any), and taxes must be shown clearly at checkout. This draft does not claim paid subscriptions are currently available.

1. Auto-renewal and authorization

Recurring subscriptions renew at the disclosed billing interval until cancellation. The checkout must disclose the recurring charge and request any authorization required by law and the applicable marketplace. Taxes, plan changes, promotional conditions and future charges must be communicated as applicable.

2. Cancel anytime

Customers may request cancellation at any time without a separate standard cancellation penalty. For auto-renewing subscriptions, cancellation stops future renewals when processed before the applicable renewal deadline. Cancellation ordinarily allows access for the remaining paid period unless the purchaser requests an available immediate termination, receives a refund requiring revocation, or lawful restrictions apply.

3. How to cancel

  • Google Play purchases: open Google Play, select Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select IMG LIVE Studio, then Cancel subscription. The Google account used for purchase must be selected. Google Play's official subscription cancellation guide explains the process. An accessible subscription management link should also appear in the app if Google Play billing is implemented.
  • Website or other direct purchases, if later enabled: cancel through the billing/account portal identified at checkout or contact imgglobal@imgofficial.com for assistance. The exact verified self-service cancellation method must be supplied before accepting direct subscriptions.

Uninstalling the app, disconnecting Facebook/YouTube, or deleting local recordings does not by itself cancel billing.

4. Refund policy

IMG does not offer a voluntary money-back guarantee. Except where applicable law, the relevant payment platform's rules, or an expressly approved exception requires or authorizes otherwise, subscription fees are non-refundable, including for changed minds and unused time. This is not a blanket “no refunds” waiver.

5. Eligible remedies and disputed payments

Verified duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, material service defects, billing errors, and other circumstances covered by mandatory law or payment rules will be investigated and remedied as required. Eligible remedies may include correction, re-performance, a partial or full refund, or other statutory relief. A customer may use the marketplace's dispute process where relevant.

6. Failed payments, renewals and account access

Access may be suspended or end if payment fails, the paid period expires, or unlawful use requires proportionate restriction. Any grace period, retry policy, change in tier, cancellation date and entitlement changes will follow the actual billing provider's rules and applicable law.

7. Regional consumer rights

Customers may have additional rights, including withdrawal/cooling-off rights and remedies for defective digital services. For example, some EU consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right subject to legal exceptions and specific consent requirements. These rights cannot be removed simply by labeling a transaction non-refundable. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable consumer protections.

8. Assistance

Include a transaction ID (not a password or full payment-card number) and purchase channel when contacting imgglobal@imgofficial.com. Customers should not send banking credentials. The timing and provider-specific method for any refund will be communicated as applicable.
