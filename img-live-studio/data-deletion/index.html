IMG LIVE Studio — User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can disconnect Facebook Login, remove saved server-side account connections, or request help deleting personal information associated with IMG LIVE Studio. This process is available to users of personal Facebook profiles and Pages alike.

Option 1 — Disconnect in IMG LIVE Studio

  1. Open IMG LIVE Studio on your Android device.
  2. Go to your connected accounts or broadcast destination settings.
  3. Choose the connected Facebook or YouTube account and tap Disconnect. Follow the confirmation if one is shown.
  4. If available, use Sign Out to remove the entire server-side IMG account session and its provider connections.

Our account backend is designed to remove the provider connection record when you disconnect; signing out removes the server session and associated account records. This does not delete publicly shared Facebook or YouTube videos or recordings stored separately on your phone.

Option 2 — Remove access in Facebook

  1. Sign in to Facebook and open Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find IMG LIVE Studio in your connected apps.
  3. Choose Remove to revoke Facebook's authorization.
  4. For deletion of any remaining IMG LIVE Studio server-side information, also disconnect in the app or contact us using the email below.

Removing the Facebook authorization does not necessarily erase copies of content stored on Facebook or all information retained on our independent account service.

Option 3 — Email a deletion request

If you can no longer access IMG LIVE Studio, or want us to assist with deleting account connection data, email mikeegoa2@gmail.com with the subject IMG LIVE Studio Data Deletion Request.

Tell us whether you connected Facebook, YouTube or both, and include only non-sensitive details needed to find and verify your request. We may ask for additional proof of authorization, because our account sessions do not require users to give us an email address. Do not send your Facebook or Google password, verification codes, app secrets or access tokens.

We will review your request, explain any information needed for verification, and delete applicable records as soon as reasonably practicable, subject to legal or security requirements. If we cannot identify an anonymous account session from the information available, we'll explain how to disconnect or revoke it securely.

What deletion covers

  • Active IMG account session credentials and associated connected-provider tokens and metadata that we can verify belong to the requested session.
  • Outstanding authorization/pairing records associated with a deleted session.
  • Expired connection and authorization records in routine cleanup.

To delete video files, replays, logos or saved settings held only on your Android device, use the app's delete controls or Android's app-storage controls where appropriate. Uninstalling the app may remove private app files. Files saved to shared device storage may require separate deletion. For broadcasts already published to Facebook or YouTube, manage deletion on those platforms.

Privacy policy and questions

See the IMG LIVE Studio Privacy Policy for details of what information we process and why. Email mikeegoa2@gmail.com with any questions.
