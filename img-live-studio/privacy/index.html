IMG LIVE Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy applies to IMG LIVE Studio, our Android sports broadcasting and recording application, including its Facebook Login, YouTube connection and related secure account services. It does not replace the separate IMG sports website privacy and advertising policy.

Information we process

  • Connected accounts: When you choose to connect Facebook or YouTube, their official authorization systems provide us an access token and possibly limited account/channel information needed to authenticate and verify the connection. At present, our Facebook Login integration requests the basic public_profile permission. We do not ask for your Facebook password.
  • App usage and broadcasting: Team and league details, scores, stream preferences, images and logos, and locally created videos or instant replay clips may be stored on your Android device. If you choose a streaming destination, authorized video and audio are transmitted to that platform when the feature is available.
  • Service operation: Short-lived authorization requests, encrypted session credentials, connection state and limited technical information necessary for operating and protecting the account service. Our hosting and networking providers may process technical data such as IP addresses as part of delivering the service.

How we use this information

We use this information to authenticate a user-requested Facebook or YouTube connection, maintain and secure their session, enable supported broadcast and recording features, troubleshoot connection failures, prevent unauthorized access and respond to account/privacy requests. We do not sell Facebook Login information or use your provider access tokens for advertising.

Facebook and YouTube permissions

You choose whether to authorize a provider. A Facebook Login connection by itself does not grant permission to publish a livestream. Facebook Live features depend on Meta's supported APIs, your account eligibility and any required approval. IMG LIVE Studio is currently being prepared and tested for that integration; features not yet enabled are not represented as active.

If a streaming feature is used, Facebook or YouTube separately processes live video and audio under its own privacy terms. See Meta's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Security and retention

Our account backend uses HTTPS and stores provider tokens encrypted at rest. App-to-server account sessions can last up to 30 days; a provider's token may expire earlier. Expired authorization/session records are scheduled for cleanup. Stored locally on your phone, saved MP4 recordings, images, replays and preferences are separate from the server account session and may remain until you delete them or uninstall the app, depending on Android storage location and app behavior.

We limit account access to what is necessary for the service. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing and service providers

We use infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare for secure account and session services, to operate the app. When you authorize a stream, the destination platform receives data necessary to provide that livestream. We do not intentionally disclose provider access tokens to other app users or include them in the app's web interface.

Your choices and data deletion

You can disconnect a connected Facebook or YouTube account from IMG LIVE Studio. That removes its account connection from our active session. Signing out removes the server-side session and its connected provider account records. You can also revoke the app from Facebook's Apps and Websites settings.

To request deletion of information related to your IMG LIVE Studio connection, or if you cannot access the app, follow our User Data Deletion Instructions. Deleting an account connection does not automatically erase separate recordings stored on your device or delete copies of a broadcast controlled by Facebook or YouTube.

Updates to this policy

As features change, we may update this policy. Significant changes will be reflected in the effective date or other notices when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion questions, email mikeegoa2@gmail.com. Please never send us your Facebook password, login code or provider access tokens.
