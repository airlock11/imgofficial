This proposed policy explains data lifecycle controls for IMG LIVE Studio. It must be read with the existing Privacy Policy and User Data Deletion Instructions. A verified server-side retention inventory is required before publication.

1. Data minimization

IMGGlobal should process only the account, connection, technical and broadcast-related information needed for disclosed and lawful purposes. Data is not retained indefinitely merely because it was collected.

2. Local content

Videos, replay clips, team/league images, scores and preferences created and stored solely on the Android device remain there until users delete them or Android storage behavior removes them, depending on the actual storage path. Removing a server connection does not automatically delete locally saved files. Users should verify local and shared-storage copies before disposing of a device.

3. Connection and session records

Existing IMG LIVE Studio documentation states that app-to-server account sessions can last up to 30 days and that expired records are scheduled for cleanup. A connected provider's token may expire sooner, and disconnect/sign-out should revoke relevant active IMG LIVE Studio connection records. Actual deletion behavior and retention of backups, security events and logs require operational verification.

4. Livestream destinations and service providers

Videos published to YouTube, Facebook or other authorized destinations are subject to those providers' retention, deletion and account policies. Requests to IMGGlobal cannot guarantee deletion of independently held third-party copies. Hosting, security and network providers may process technical metadata under their own contracts and applicable law.

5. Future subscription and financial records

If payment subscriptions are launched, IMGGlobal will publish appropriate disclosures about payment provider, invoices, transaction identifiers, legal retention and deletion. IMGGlobal should avoid storing full payment card numbers in its own systems. No payment-provider integration or specific retention duration is asserted in this draft.

6. Access, correction and deletion requests

Submit a privacy request to imgglobal@imgofficial.com or use available app controls. IMGGlobal may reasonably verify authorization before disclosing or deleting information. Legal exceptions, backup restoration cycles, security investigations and valid retention obligations may affect the timing or scope of a request; IMGGlobal must explain lawful limitations.

7. Security and incident response

IMGGlobal should maintain reasonable technical and organizational security measures, access restrictions and an incident response process. If a breach triggers a legal notification requirement, the affected authorities and users must be notified within applicable deadlines. No blanket guarantee of absolute security is made.

8. Required retention inventory before release

Review gate: document each server and device data category, purpose, jurisdiction, processor/subprocessor, retention period, backup/log duration, deletion trigger, and verified deletion test. The final published policy should replace this review gate with concrete, accurate periods where required.
