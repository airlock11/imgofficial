Broadcasting & Content Policy

Pre-publication draft prepared November 3, 2026. No effective date has been set.

DRAFT FOR LEGAL AND OPERATIONAL REVIEW — NOT YET EFFECTIVE. Do not publish until the service operator, billing arrangements, platform capabilities and relevant laws have been verified.

IMG LIVE Studio provides production tools, not a license to record or transmit third-party events. This draft supplements the Global Terms of Service.

1. Rights and approvals

Before recording or streaming, the broadcaster must hold or obtain all applicable permissions for the match, venue, teams, event organizer, athletes, music, trademarks, logos, copyrighted material, and distribution platform. Permission to attend an event is not necessarily permission to broadcast it.

2. Minors and spectators

Broadcasters must satisfy applicable child-safeguarding, consent, notice, and image/privacy requirements for young athletes and other people shown or heard. IMG features must not be used for unlawful or invasive surveillance. User acknowledgments do not replace legal consent obligations.

3. Responsibility for content and publishing

Users are responsible for selecting privacy and audience settings, checking their stream preview, controlling camera/microphone capture, verifying the destination, and deciding what becomes publicly available. A connected YouTube or Facebook account does not itself establish broadcast authorization.

4. Sports scoring and remote controllers

Account holders must authorize remote scoring participants and verify official match outcomes independently. Scoreboards, replay graphics, timing tools and calculations are broadcast aids, not a guarantee of official results or refereeing accuracy.

5. Prohibited content

Do not broadcast unlawful, infringing, exploitative, threatening, harassing or deceptive material, or content prohibited by applicable destination-platform rules. Do not bypass platform security, misuse third-party accounts, or falsely claim IMG endorsement.

6. Reports, takedowns and appeals

Rights-holders and affected users may report concerns to imgglobal@imgofficial.com. Provide a description, relevant URLs if any, and a way to contact you without sending sensitive passwords. IMG may investigate and take lawful, proportionate action. Formal statutory copyright notice and counter-notice procedures must be completed if and when applicable to the service.

7. External platforms and continuity

Streaming availability depends on device capability, Internet connectivity, external APIs, account eligibility and destination-platform policies. Users should maintain independent recordings or fallback systems for critical events. IMG remains responsible for its own duties where law requires.
