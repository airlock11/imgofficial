Privacy & Advertising

Last updated: September 22, 2026

IMG operates imgofficial.com as a sports information platform. This page explains how the site may use technical data, cookies, analytics and advertising services.

Information the site may process

Like most websites, IMG may process basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, approximate location derived from network information, referring pages and interaction data needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

Cookies and local storage

IMG may use cookies or browser storage for site preferences, measurement, security and advertising. Some features, such as theme preferences, can use local browser storage.

Advertising

IMG may use Google AdSense or other advertising partners to display advertisements. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and improve ads, subject to applicable law and user consent requirements.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and consent requirements. Where required, IMG will use an appropriate consent management platform before personalized advertising is served.

Analytics

IMG may use analytics services to understand traffic and improve the site. Analytics settings may be adjusted as the platform develops.

Third-party content and links

IMG may link to or embed third-party services, including sports data providers, video platforms and news sources. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

Where applicable, visitors may be offered controls for advertising and privacy choices. Browser settings can also be used to manage cookies and site data.

Contact

For privacy or advertising questions, contact imgglobal@imgofficial.com.
